## Scenarios

Each use case covers a different clinical context and highlights a different set of calculator features.

### [Carrier Screening Counseling](/use-cases/carrier-screening)

A couple planning pregnancy where one partner is a known CFTR carrier. Demonstrates searching for a gene, reviewing the variant table, and excluding a variant with disputed pathogenicity (c.1210-11T>G).

**Key features:** Variant table, individual variant exclusion, heterozygous carrier risk.

### [Recurrence Risk and Family Planning](/use-cases/family-planning)

Parents of a child with cystic fibrosis want to understand the risk for future pregnancies. Demonstrates compound heterozygous status and how the recurrence risk calculation differs from a carrier scenario.

**Key features:** Compound heterozygous status, risk divisor differences, population-specific values.

### [Clinical Letter Generation](/use-cases/clinical-letter)

A patient carries an HFE variant and needs a clinical letter. Demonstrates selective variant inclusion, German text generation with gender-inclusive language, and template customization.

**Key features:** Selective variant inclusion, German output, three clinical perspectives, template sections.
