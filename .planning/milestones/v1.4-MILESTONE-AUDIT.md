### Phase 23: Wizard Advancement Interpretation
ROADMAP success criterion 1 says "pre-fills the gene search **and advances the wizard**." The implementation pre-fills CFTR and enables Continue but does not auto-advance to Step 2. The requirement UXO-02 only says "pre-fills gene search" which is satisfied. This is a design choice (letting the user confirm before advancing) rather than a gap.
