## Step 2: Set the Patient Status (GUID-04)

Explain all 4 patient status options with clinical context:
- **Heterozygous carrier**: The index patient carries one pathogenic variant. This is the most common scenario in carrier screening — the partner's carrier risk determines recurrence risk. Risk divisor: carrier_frequency / 4.
- **Homozygous affected**: The patient has two copies of the same pathogenic variant. Risk divisor: carrier_frequency / 2.
- **Compound heterozygous (confirmed)**: Two different pathogenic variants confirmed on separate alleles (by parental testing or long-read sequencing). Risk divisor: carrier_frequency / 2.
- **Compound heterozygous (assumed)**: Two different pathogenic variants detected but phase not confirmed. Same risk calculation as confirmed, but note the assumption. Risk divisor: carrier_frequency / 2.

::: info Why Does Patient Status Matter?
The status determines the recurrence risk formula. For a heterozygous carrier, the risk depends on the partner also being a carrier (probability = carrier frequency) AND both passing on their pathogenic allele (1/4). For affected individuals, one allele is already confirmed pathogenic, so the divisor is 1/2 instead.
:::

## Step 3: Choose a Frequency Source (GUID-05)

Explain the three tabs:
- **gnomAD tab** (default): Queries gnomAD directly for allele frequencies of pathogenic variants in the selected gene. The variant table shows all qualifying variants with their allele frequencies. You can review, include, or exclude individual variants. Link to [Filters](/reference/filters) for how variants are selected.
- **Literature tab**: Enter a carrier frequency from a published study. Useful when you have a known population-specific value. Enter the frequency and optionally a PubMed ID for citation in the generated text.
- **Default tab**: Uses a fallback carrier frequency of 1% (1:100). Useful for quick estimates when neither gnomAD data nor literature values are available.

## Step 4: Review Results and Generate Text (GUID-06)

Explain the results view:
